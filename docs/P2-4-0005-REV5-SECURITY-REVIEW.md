## 6. Remaining risks and unverified assumptions (consolidated)
1. **INV-TX / P1** (above): if a client can cause a rollback after a returned result, refusals, audited reads and throttle counters leave no trace. Mutations do not escape (they roll back with their trace).
2. **Manual Dashboard removal** can hit the wrong user; detected, not prevented. The attestation proves only that the named owner has no verified factor and the count matches.
3. **Stranded owner** has no instant in-system path beyond another window within the same request, a new request (quota) or break-glass; availability risk of up to about a week.
4. **A race for the enrolment** inside the window: whoever enrols and verifies first with the owner's password session wins (same as rev4); mitigated by the live session with the owner, the short window and the cool-off notice. Not removable.
5. **Audit volume**: refusals are audited and unbounded per operator (application throttle P3 reduces it for application callers only).
6. **Account deletion is blocked** for any user with history until the erasure procedure exists.
7. Operators with the capability can open windows for any owner that satisfies the checks; the control is two people plus the monthly review, not the database.
8. **Policy-only items:** that the two accounts are two people; that verification happened and was strong; that the independent channel was independent; that notifications were read.
9. **Hosted-platform unknowns:** PostgREST configuration, Auth behavior under the real removal, platform defaults on `db-tx-end`.

## 7. Decisions needed from the project owner (nothing was assumed)
1. Accept **no quota exemption** for stranded owners (rev5 keeps 2 per owner per 7 days), or approve a reviewed exemption design.
2. Accept the **window allowance** (3 before, 6 after an attested removal) and the 60 minute window length.
3. The **application throttle numbers** (30 per operator per hour, 15 per target per hour, 12 and 40 per hour for codes) and the choice of shared store.
4. The **erasure procedure** (needed before any account deletion for owners with history) and its retention numbers.
5. The **break-glass emergency allowance migration** (designed only on first real need) and who the two deciders are.
6. If INV-TX fails: the order of the remedies (platform setting, edge block, authoritative application throttle, a separately reviewed dblink design).
7. Whether the removal should stay a manual Dashboard step (current) or be automated later through an audited server-side Admin API call (a different security design).
